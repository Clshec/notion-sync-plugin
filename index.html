Alf Notion Super Productivity

Notion plans. Super Productivity executes.

Development foundation

Notion synchronization disabled

This preview deliberately does not connect to Notion, request credentials, or write data. The inherited alpha sync engine is unsafe for general-purpose Notion databases. A configurable, non-destructive replacement is being developed.

Your existing Notion workspace and Super Productivity / SuperSync data are not changed by this preview.

What the integration will support

  • Existing Notion databases: configurable project, task, bundle and habit mappings without creating new databases.
  • Quick planning fields: schedule day/time, separate deadline, estimate, completion status and priority.
  • Time tracking: verified actual time flowing from Super Productivity to Notion.
  • Pomodoro, Flowtime and Countdown: real completed session counts after a reliable session API is available.
  • Habits: daily counters, streaks and original Notion habit definitions.
  • Offline-first: desktop and Android execution with conflict-safe syncing.
